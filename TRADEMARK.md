# exist.io Trademark Policy

This document outlines the policy regarding the use of the "exist.io" name, logos, and related branding assets.

## 1. Purpose
The open-source code for exist.io is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. However, open-source software licenses do not grant trademark rights. This policy ensures that users, developers, and operators know whether a deployment, product, or service originates directly from the official exist.io project or from an independent third party.

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Permission Required)
You may use the exist.io wordmark without explicit written permission to:
- Accurately describe that your software, plugin, or agent interfaces with or supports exist.io (e.g. *"MCP connector for exist.io"*, *"Runs on exist.io"*).
- Link to the official repository (`https://github.com/RavaniRoshan/exist.io`) or official website (`https://ravaniroshan.github.io/exist.io/`).
- State truthfully that your service or self-hosted cluster runs the exist.io open-source codebase.

## 3. Restricted Uses (Requires Written Permission)
You may **not** use the exist.io name or logo in any of the following manners without prior written consent:
- In the name of a commercial product, SaaS platform, or hosted service (e.g., you cannot launch a hosted service called *"exist.io Cloud"* or *"exist.io Hosted"*).
- In any domain name, subdomain, or business entity name.
- In any way that falsely implies official sponsorship, endorsement, or direct association with the maintainers of exist.io.
- Modifying, distorting, or altering the official exist.io logo marks.

## 4. Inquiries
For questions regarding trademark usage or permission requests, please contact `maintainers@exist.io`.
